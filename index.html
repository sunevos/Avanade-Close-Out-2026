//About the program

The AI to Opportunity Program is Goodwall's flagship digital skilling initiative, delivered in partnership with the NextGen AI Alliance. It was designed to scale AI literacy, practical AI skills and workforce readiness among young people worldwide. At the heart of the program is AI Fundamentals, a mobile-first learning path that takes young people from AI curiosity to demonstrated, credentialed AI skills. Learners watch short, creator-led videos, put AI tools like Microsoft Copilot and the Goodwall AI Companion into practice through hands-on challenges, and return to the Goodwall app to show what they have learned. It is the main entry point into a wider set of AI to Opportunity learning paths co-designed with NextGen AI Alliance partners.

AI Fundamentals is the foundational AI course of the AI to Opportunity program, originally built with Microsoft, HP and Cognizant and available to youth worldwide on Goodwall. In this pilot program, Avanade brought the course to Brazilian youth in their own language.

The Portuguese course, Fundamentos da IA, takes complete beginners from "what is AI" to writing prompts and generating images with AI, then asks them to show what they learned in a video challenge. It's mobile-first, free, presented by Brazilian Gen Z creators, and needs no coding or tech background. Top challenge entries earned cash rewards and HP laptops.

The pilot launched in-app on 10 June 2026.

//Program objectives

Localize AI learning for Brazil

Translate and re-produce an AI learning path and challenge in Brazilian Portuguese, with cultural relevance and accessibility built in.

Expand access through local networks

Reach Brazilian youth through Avanade's Tech Hubs, universities, youth ambassadors and Goodwall's social learning community.

Showcase Avanade's leadership

Bring Brazilian youth stories to global convenings such as UNGA and Davos, as evidence of inclusive innovation.

//Program context

The NextGen AI Alliance is a coalition founded by Microsoft, HP and Cognizant, and powered by Goodwall, to scale AI skills for youth worldwide. Its goal is 10 million youth accessing AI skilling opportunities by 2027. Avanade joined as a Network Partner to lead a focused Brazil pilot and local activation of the Alliance's foundational AI content.

Brazil was chosen because underserved young people there have little access to AI tools and training, and almost none in Brazilian Portuguese.

//Impact

The pilot closed at 105% of its Reached target, 108% of Engaged, 124% of Trained and over 15 times its Credentialed target, in under four months live.

Four months instead of twelve

The program was co-designed in September 2025 for a November launch, but partner onboarding took longer than planned. The course went live on 10 June 2026, one month after final contracting was approved. The team compressed a twelve-month plan into four months.

//Summary

The Avanade Brazil pilot put a full AI Fundamentals course in Portuguese in front of Brazilian youth for the first time. In under four months it reached 3.1 million young people, trained 24,738 and credentialed 4,638, above target on every KPI.

//What worked

1

Full-path localization

All four modules, badges and the certificate were in Portuguese, presented by Brazilian creators. Learners could finish the whole course in their own language. That's the main difference from earlier single-module translations, and it shows in the credentialing result.

2

Hands-on credentials

Every badge asked learners to do something with AI: answer a quiz, send a prompt, generate an image, record a story. Credentialed passed its target within three weeks.

3

Avanade's network and visibility

Avanade employees helped shape the localized content, and Brazilian youth stories featured in the NextGen AI Alliance roundtable and partner insights at UNGA 81. Avanade also shared the program with its partners and interns, and we gave them partner and youth briefing materials in Portuguese. This had a clear effect on the quality of youth participation.

//Areas for improvement

1

Late start

The pilot was co-designed for November 2025 and launched in June 2026. Four months live left little time for ambassador and Tech Hub activation.

2

We need more ways to activate local networks

Content was ready in June, but Tech Hub outreach and the STEM Scholar ambassador program were still building at close. Where Avanade shared the course directly with partners and interns, participation quality rose. The next round needs more channels to reach campuses, youth organizations and ambassadors, set up from week one.

3

The referral campaign wasn't promoted beyond the Goodwall app

It produced only two to three referrals. Invited youth tend to stay longer and complete more of the learning path, so this is worth reviewing and promoting across networks in Year 2.

//Recommendations

Connect learning to Avanade jobs

Use Avanade talent acquisition's dedicated application link inside the learner journey: alongside the certificate, as a final course step, or through Goodwall's Career Compass. This makes employability measurable, in line with Avanade's Responsible Business focus.

Run the referral campaign properly

Relaunch it with in-app promotion and a quarterly push through Avanade's intern network.

Refresh network activation with new youth- and partner-ready assets in Portuguese

Brief Tech Hubs, universities and STEM Scholar ambassadors with refreshed information assets.

Track youth after the course

Add self-reported progress at 3, 6 and 9 months to show what learners do with their new skills.

Closing

Avanade set out to give Brazilian youth practical AI skills in their own language. Over 24,000 young people took that step, and more than 4,600 proved what they learned. The next step is to link those skills to real jobs, and Avanade is well placed to do it.
